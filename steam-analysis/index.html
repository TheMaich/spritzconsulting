Common questions

Questions, before you order.

The questions I get most before an order. If yours isn’t here, send it through the contact form and I answer before you order.

Which tier is right for me?

Solo Dev Tier is free, for students, hobbyists and first-time developers who want to understand what the numbers mean.

Indie Tier is the complete analysis, for indie studios, experienced developers and anyone who wants to analyse a competitor’s game.

Publisher Tier is for publishing decisions: scouting a game, understanding what publishing involves, or judging how real a competitor’s threat is.

Is the Solo Dev Tier really free?

The Solo Dev Tier is free. Each email gets a maximum of 3 analyses, and each one is a lighter version with five slides, no Steam page review and no market visibility section.

Do you use AI in this analysis?

Yes. AI helps me gather the public data and run the first pass of the numbers, which keeps delivery fast and the price fair. The method and the final checks are mine, and every number is verified against its source before you see it.

How do you estimate wishlists, and how accurate is it?

From your page’s own history: how fast followers have grown, and what that pace implies up to your launch date. If you give me your real wishlist count (for your own game), I start from it. The result is an estimate, shown as a cautious, central and optimistic outcome, with every assumption listed so you can see what would change it.

What are the sources of the projections? How reliable are they?

The sources are public: your Steam store page, its follower count and growth history, public trackers such as SteamDB, and the review counts of comparable games. The methods are the ones widely used across the industry: the Boxleiter method, which estimates sales from review counts, and follower-to-wishlist ratios applied to your page’s own growth trend. Nothing private is needed. If you share your real wishlist count, I use it in place of the estimate.

It is a grounded estimate that tells you reliably where the game stands and how it compares with similar games. It is less exact on sales: marketing, timing and the game itself can move the result well beyond any model. That is why the forecast comes as scenarios, cautious, middle and optimistic, with the assumptions behind them written down. Your real wishlist number tightens them the most.

Is my analysis private?

Each analysis is made for the person who orders it and is not shared. It uses only public data, so you can order one for any game.

When should I order the analysis?

Ideally 6 to 12 months before launch, once your Steam page has been live for a few weeks. By then there is enough history to read the trend, and still time to act on it: enter a festival, release a demo or move the date. Closer to launch it still tells you whether your wishlists are enough for the date you picked. If your game is already out, order whenever you like: the analysis shows how it has sold so far, how its sales hold up over time and how it compares with similar games. You can also order it for a competitor’s game: the analysis needs only a public Steam page, so you see how that game is doing, how fast it is growing and what it is likely to sell. If you work in publishing, order whenever a game lands on your radar, before you talk to the team or commit to a deal.
