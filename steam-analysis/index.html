Method and limits

How the numbers are built

Everything comes from public data: your Steam page, the community hub, review histories and SteamDB. I do not see your Steamworks data.

Observed

Followers, page age, reviews, prices and release dates.

Modelled

Wishlists, copies sold and revenue. Wishlists follow your page’s own growth, not a fixed multiplier on followers. Copies for other games come from their review counts (about 30 copies per review).

Gross

Revenue is gross: at list price, before Steam’s cut, refunds, taxes and discounts.

These are estimates, not forecasts you can bank. With your real wishlist numbers and plans, the picture gets sharper.

Questions

FAQ

Do you need access to my Steamworks account?

No. The analysis uses only public data. If you want to share your real wishlist count, tell me in the form and I use it.

How do you estimate wishlists?

From your page’s own history: how fast followers have grown since it went live, and what that pace implies up to the launch date. Today’s wishlists are never reduced. I state the assumptions on the slide.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate built from public information. The notes in the deck show every assumption, so you can see what would change the result.

What does gross revenue mean?

Copies times the price, before Steam’s 30% cut, refunds, taxes and sales discounts.

What if my game is already released?

For now I only analyse games that have not launched yet. Write to me if yours is out and I’ll tell you what is possible.

How long does it take?

The free preview and the Standard deck: within 2 business days. Premium: within 3. If Steam limits my data collection, I tell you and it can take up to a day longer.

Can I get a refund?

If the deck contains a data error, I correct it. Because each deck is made to order, there are no refunds otherwise. At checkout you confirm that I start the work right away.

Is there a call included?

No. If you want to talk it through, you can book a free 30-minute discovery call or a two-hour consultation (€120).

What do you do with my data?

I use your email and Steam link only to make and send your analysis. Decks are deleted after 30 days. See the privacy policy.
