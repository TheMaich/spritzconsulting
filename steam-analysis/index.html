Common questions

Questions, before you order.

The four things developers ask most about the analysis. If yours isn’t here, send it through the contact form and I answer before you order.

Do you use AI in this analysis?

Yes. AI does the data collection and the first pass of the numbers, and that is why you get the analysis in 2 days. Done by hand, it would take about 2 weeks. The method and the checks are mine: I review every Premium deck and a sample of Standard decks.

Do you need access to my Steamworks account?

No. The analysis uses only public data. If you want to share your real wishlist count, tell me when you order and I use it.

How do you estimate wishlists, and how accurate is it?

From your page’s own history: how fast followers have grown since it went live, and what that pace implies up to the launch date. Today’s wishlists are never reduced. It is an estimate built from public information, and the notes in the deck show every assumption, so you can see what would change the result.

Can I get a refund?

If the deck contains a data error, I correct it. Because each deck is made to order, there are no refunds otherwise. At checkout you confirm that I start the work right away.
