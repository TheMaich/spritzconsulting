Common questions

Questions, before you order.

The four things developers ask most about the analysis. If yours isn’t here, send it through the contact form and I answer before you order.

Do you use AI in this analysis?

Yes. AI does the data collection and the first pass of the numbers, and that is why you get the analysis in 2 days. Done by hand, it would take about 2 weeks. The method and the final checks are mine.

Do you need access to my Steamworks account?

No. The analysis uses only public data. If you want to share your real wishlist count, tell me when you order and I use it.

How do you estimate wishlists, and how accurate is it?

From your page’s own history: how fast followers have grown since it went live, and what that pace implies up to the launch date. Today’s wishlists are never reduced. It is an estimate built from public information, and the notes in the analysis show every assumption, so you can see what would change the result.

When should I order the analysis?

Ideally 6 to 12 months before launch, once your Steam page has been live for a few weeks. By then there is enough history to read the trend, and still time to act on it: enter a festival, release a demo or move the date. Closer to launch it still tells you whether your wishlists are enough for the date you picked. If your game is already out, order whenever you like: the analysis shows how it has sold so far, how its sales hold up over time and how it compares with similar games.
