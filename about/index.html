About

Ciao! I’m Michele

Now Independent producer · Spritz Consulting
Based Europe · CET
Engage Project · Retainer · Audit

Or Miki for short. I’m an independent game producer, and Spritz Consulting is my production and publishing consultancy. I help studios and publishers ship on schedule and within budget, from first build to release.

Studio side, publisher side, and everything in between.

I started as a playtester for Milestone and EA DICE, with part-time QA at Milestone. Then I spent ten years moving through every part of game production: QA at 34BigThings and Rebellion, production back at 34BigThings, publishing production at Raw Fury, and most recently Producer at Untold Games on City 20. In June 2026 I founded Spritz Consulting.

AAA down to one-person indie teams. QA, production, publishing. I have sat on every side of the table and it changes what you notice.

Career

  1. Jun 2026 – now

    Spritz Consulting · Founder

    Release management on PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo platforms, from onboarding and certification to launch. Delivery planning, milestone tracking and vendor selection for independent studios. Pitch reviews and mock publisher interviews before a studio meets a real one.

  2. Feb – Apr 2026

    Untold Games · Producer

    Production on City 20, a live game with a 15-person team. I owned the schedule, the roadmap and backlog, and the release plan for updates.

  3. 2022 – 2025

    Raw Fury · Quality & Line Producer, then Publishing Producer

    I owned the delivery pipeline for several titles, as the link between Raw Fury, the external developers and the platform holders: milestones, backlog, legal compliance, and the QA, localization and deployment vendors. Each title shipped in 15+ languages, with demo builds planned around international events. One title came in 10% under budget and launched to Overwhelmingly Positive user reviews. Dome Keeper, Moonstone Island, Kathy Rain 2 and Ereban: Shadow Legacy.

  4. 2021 – 2022

    34BigThings · Associate Producer

    Redout 2: the day-to-day link with the publisher, from build deliveries to milestone reviews, and certification on 7 platforms.

  5. 2018 – 2021

    Rebellion · Senior QA Tester, then Lead QA Tester

    Led QA on Sniper Elite 5, directing internal teams and external QA vendors from triage to fix. Also Sniper Elite 4, Strange Brigade and Zombie Army 4: Dead War. I designed the QA process for a new platform, which became the company-wide standard.

  6. 2016 – 2018

    34BigThings · QA Tester, then QA Manager

    Led a team of 5+ testers on Redout and Super Inefficient Golf, owning test plans, scheduling and reporting to production.

  7. Early career

    Milestone and EA DICE · Playtester, then part-time QA tester

    Playtesting for Milestone and for EA DICE in Stockholm in 2016, then part-time QA at Milestone on Ride, MXGP3 and Valentino Rossi: The Game.
